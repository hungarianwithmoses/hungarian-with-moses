Your Teacher

Hi, I'm Moses

I'm a native Hungarian speaker, a professional tutor, and a polyglot — I speak Hungarian, English, Spanish, Italian, Romanian, and Turkish. I've spent the last several years teaching Hungarian to thousands of students online, from complete beginners to people preparing for their Hungarian citizenship interview.

Through those lessons I built my own method for getting people speaking real Hungarian fast, without drowning in grammar tables first. This course is that method, distilled into 46 videos you can work through at your own pace.

I also write books on learning Hungarian and preparing for the citizenship process, and help students directly through one-on-one tutoring and mock citizenship interviews.
